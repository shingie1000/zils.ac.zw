Zimbabwe Law School

About the ZILS

The Zimbabwe Institute of Legal Studies is the country’s first and only legal/law college in the paralegal directory offering middle level qualifications in law and related specialist disciplines. It offers holders of 'O' Level qualifications an opportunity to embark on a journey to become lawyers, which journey has been the exclusive preserve of holders of 'A' qualifications. Even holders of 'A' level qualifications who have a dream of becoming lawyers but do not have sufficient points to enter university can embark on this journey and eventually realise their dreams.
