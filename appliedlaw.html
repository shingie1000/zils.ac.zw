Applied Law


NATIONAL CERTIFICATE (Duration: 8 Months)


This course will provide you with knowledge to the Zimbabwe legal system and its personnel as well as some of the main topics within the law. It provides an insight into the court structure – both civil and criminal, the ways in which law is made and elements of substantive civil and criminal law in the form of negligence and non-fatal offences to the person.

Entry Requirements

You will require 5 Odinary Level passes, including English language.

Fees

US$ 750.00 per semester.

  • there are two (2) semesters in year
  • each semester is four (4) months long
  • exams are written after each semester
  • certification after successsful completion of each module
  • National Certificate in Applied law is awarded after successful completion of all fourteen (14) modules

Careers

You will be able to work as a paralegal clerk.
